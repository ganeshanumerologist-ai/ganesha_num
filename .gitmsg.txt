Show the birth chart where the result is, and a guard for missing imports

The user said the chart still was not showing. It was, but only after a
long scroll: the result area showed the name, the birth line and the eight
numerology numbers, and nothing else. Rashi, nakshatra, gana and vara sat
further down inside the full chart, so the page read as empty.

A birth summary now sits directly under the name: lagna with its lord, sun
and moon sign with the moon's exact degree, nakshatra with pada, gana,
nadi, rashi lord, weekday with its lord, tithi with paksha, yoga and
karana. Eleven fields, one glance, before anything else.

Gana was missing from the engine entirely. Added the standard
nakshatra-to-gana table, twelve Deva, six Manushya and nine Rakshasa, plus
nadi on its three-cycle.

Two crashes fixed on the way. KundliChart called rIdx() with no import,
which blanked the whole page. And lagnaLord arrives already in Sanskrit,
so wrapping it in a second Sanskrit-to-Sanskrit map returned undefined and
two fields rendered empty.

check-missing-imports.mjs is new and runs over every source file looking
for a helper that is called but never imported. It ignores comments, which
is what made the first run report sixteen false positives.
