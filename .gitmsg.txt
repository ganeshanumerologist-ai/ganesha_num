Cleaner background, fluid 4K type, and city-aware Rahu Kaal

Two things. First, a design pass: I had been lightening the background
blind and it was still wrong, because the problem was never darkness. A
screenshot showed five competing layers at once, with the mandala rings
running straight through the headline and a clipped kalash in the corner.
Lighter is not the same as clean.

The two kundali chart layers are now removed from the background
entirely, which is where most of the line-art clutter was. The mandala
stays but drops to .11 opacity behind a radial mask, so it fades out
before it reaches the text instead of crossing it. The kalash no longer
clips. The aurora is down to three soft colours from five.

The headline gradient went from gold-pink-purple-cyan to three warm
shades. Four hues read as a rainbow rather than a brand. Hero type is
now fluid via clamp, growing from 34px on a phone to 104px at 4K; the
old rule capped at 80px and had already stopped growing by 1280px, so
on a 4K panel it looked undersized. All contrast sits at AAA.

Second, Rahu Kaal. The user was right that it was Delhi's time for
everyone. It was a fixed IST string table, so Nagpur and Chennai showed
Delhi's window. The day is now split into eight segments from the
selected city's own sunrise, giving ten different Rahu Kaal windows
across ten cities. Delhi computes 16:38-18:04 against the published IST
standard of 16:30-18:00, which is the expected agreement.
