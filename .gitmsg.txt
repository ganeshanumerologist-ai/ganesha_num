Recommendations instead of bare lists in the full Kundli chart

The user pointed out four gaps in "Your complete Kundli": the varshphal
did not say which year it belonged to, the Lal Kitab remedies were one
line each, the gemstone section listed every stone without saying which
one to wear, and the auspicious section named muhurtas but gave no times.

Varshpal now states the calendar year and the running personal year up
front and puts the running one first with a badge. Lal Kitab gives each
planet seven fields: result in that house, likely troubles, donation,
worship, mantra with count, best time, and the base remedy, each sorted
by whether it runs in the current dasha or the planet is weak.

Gemstones are now a decision. Ranking weighs ashtakavarga points,
shadbala, benefic or malefic nature, and kendra-trikona placement.
Shadow planets are marked do-not-wear, as are the loss and disease
houses. A debilitated planet is only optional, since a stone unsettles
it further. Cap is three, one at a time. If nothing qualifies the top
candidate is promoted so there is always an answer.

Auspicious and inauspicious times are computed, not listed. Abhijit,
Brahma, Vijay, Godhuli, Amrit, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika, Bhadra and
Panchak all derive from the birth city sunrise and sunset, so they move
with the city.

While checking the render I found three English leaks. grahaName keyed
its table by Sanskrit names while the astro engine returns English, so
no planet was ever translated on any page. RATNA data was English only.
Muhurat names and the gem rationale were hardcoded. All three are now
translation keys: 201 keys per language across ten languages, with the
remaining twelve falling back to a same-script relative.

check-kf-files.mjs exempts the {p} and {h} placeholders, which it was
flagging as Latin, and still catches real script intrusions. It found two
Devanagari characters that had crept into Tamil and Telugu values.
